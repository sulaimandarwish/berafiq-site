BeRafiq-specific application code, designs, pricing models, configuration and documentation are proprietary. All rights reserved by their respective rights holders. No open-source license is granted for BeRafiq-authored work.

Do not publish new proprietary source, pricing configuration, calculation traces or internal tools to public repositories or public hosting. Use private repositories and access-controlled internal tools for future development and review.

Third-party software remains subject to its own licenses. This policy does not replace upstream licenses or remove required notices, and does not claim exclusive ownership of third-party components.

## Current access limitations

This notice records the owner's source policy; it does not make existing files private or revoke rights already granted under applicable terms. At the time this notice was added, this repository and its GitHub Pages deployment were public. Browser-delivered JavaScript, including the estimator, can be downloaded by visitors. The calculation-review page is also public.

To protect confidential implementation details:
- Change repository visibility to private after confirming continued hosting support.
- Move proprietary pricing logic and rates into a private backend.
- Require server-enforced authentication and authorization for calculation review and internal data.
- Deploy only the public customer interface and necessary assets.
- Preserve third-party license obligations.

Hiding links, adding noindex, minifying JavaScript or using a client-side password does not provide access control.
